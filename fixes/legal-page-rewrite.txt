Three-stage system for legal landing and practice-area pages: Stage 1 verification blueprint, Stage 2 execution draft, Stage 3 critique/audit (can run standalone). Optimizes for Google rankings, LLM citations, CRO, and SSIT gate survival. Trigger on rewrite/audit/critique of a page ONLY when the page belongs to a law firm or the context is legal — all legal content is YMYL/high-stakes. Do NOT use for legal blog posts or articles (geo-master-blueprint) or WordPress HTML builds (wp-classic-designer).
