Score content outputs against a numeric rubric using a judge model, aggregate across a frozen golden set, and diff one version against a prior one to catch silent regressions. Trigger on: score a batch of articles or pages, run a scored eval, judge outputs against a rubric, "did v-N+1 beat v-N", regression check on a content version, build or run a golden set. Ships GEO, legal, and PPC rubrics (--rubric); runs on any OpenRouter model. Measures whether a content system improved, not just changed.
